# Methods and limitations

Observed country series are CCKP-supplied area aggregates of ERA5 at 0.25° resolution. The displayed recent temperature difference compares the 2015–2025 mean with the 1991–2020 mean. CMIP6 panels show anomalies for 2020–2039, 2040–2059 and 2080–2099, labelled 2030, 2050 and 2100. Ensemble median and P10–P90 are retained separately for SSP1–2.6, SSP2–4.5 and SSP5–8.5.

Glacier results are NASA PyGEM-OGGM percentage anomalies relative to 2000. They exclude ice sheets and are displayed only when the official CCKP country aggregate returns data.

Country-level coastal projections require a verified EEZ identifier. Land ISO codes are not silently mapped to EEZs. Subnational analysis requires CCKP ADM1 identifiers and is kept separate from national means. Snow-cover depth or duration is not substituted by frost days; no snow indicator is published unless a reproducible country aggregate is available.

No missing value is interpolated. No composite score is constructed. Co-movement is not causal attribution. National values do not represent local topography, cities or individual exposure.
