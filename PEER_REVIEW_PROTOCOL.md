# Independent scientific review protocol

Status at release: awaiting independent external review.

Reviewers should disclose expertise and conflicts of interest and assess:

1. Dataset identity, version, licensing and citation accuracy.
2. Correct parsing of ERA5, CMIP6 and PyGEM-OGGM products.
3. Reference periods, units, aggregation and anomaly definitions.
4. Correct representation of P10, median and P90.
5. Missing-value handling and reproducibility from supplied URLs.
6. Separation of observations, projections and assessed statements.
7. Absence of unsupported event attribution or composite risk scoring.
8. Country-average and spatial-resolution limitations.
9. Accessibility, export consistency and machine-readable provenance.
10. Whether every public claim is supported by an identified evidence artifact.

The reviewer should return a signed report containing major issues, minor issues, recommendation and date. Reports and author responses should be archived as a new version; review status must not be marked “reviewed” before this occurs.
