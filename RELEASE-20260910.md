# Climate 2.1.0 — 10 September 2026

Published to the existing Flask service and URL. No site migration or global navigation changes.

## Changes

- Compact English explanations for emissions pathways (SSP), climate-model ensembles (CMIP6), historical estimates (ERA5), percentiles, baselines, and physical units. Additional definitions are collapsible.
- IPCC temperature ranges labelled very likely, for the 2081–2100 average; sea-level ranges kept distinct. Export upper bound corrected to 1.01 m and 2050 cross-scenario range to 0.15–0.29 m.
- NSIDC official September monthly product replaces averaging daily data. Verified 1979–2025 coverage; incomplete September cannot be published.
- Per-source success timestamps, fallback status and data-period labels. One global-series failure retains that series rather than cancelling all four.
- Country profiles renew asynchronously on access after seven days, suppress duplicate refreshes, throttle retries, and retain the cached profile if observations fail or projection completeness decreases.
- Country projection reference is explicitly 1995–2014; recent historical temperature comparison remains 2015–2025 versus 1991–2020.
- Historical country chart now has labelled units and scales, a line rather than stretched bars, and an accessible annual-value table.
- Glacier source was tested directly: CHE area at 2000 = 100; SSP5–8.5 at 2050 = 37.75 and 2100 = 0.87. Despite the API product name `anomalypercent`, these values form an index relative to 2000. No addition of 100 or other numerical conversion applied. Reader-facing label explains the index.
- Rankings identify loaded time coverage. Heat-country comparison explicitly changes both time and scenario, and is not population-weighted exposure.
- Print/PDF action removed from this dashboard; CSV, JSON and citation exports retained.
- Service changelog endpoint updated to version 2.1.0.
